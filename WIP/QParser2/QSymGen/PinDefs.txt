* ------------------------------------------------------------------
* Example Pin Definition File for QSymGen
* ------------------------------------------------------------------
*
* Pin Definitions:  DIR NAME [NET] [* COMMENT]
*
* Where: DIR = Pin direction: I (input) or O (output).  (Not case-sensitive.)
*        NAME = Pin name.  (Duplicates are allowed.)
*        NET = Optional net name.  (If supplied, the pin will be present but hidden.)
*        COMMENT = Optional comment.  (Space required before '*'.)
*
* ---------------------------------
*
* Notes:
*   Lines beginning with '*' are comments and are ignored.
*   Blank lines are ignored.
*
* ---------------------------------
*
* Example Pin Definitions:
*
* I VREF VREFNET * A reference voltage input net
I VREF
I ADC
I RA0
I RA1
I RA2
O DAC
O RB0
O RB1
O RB2
O RB3
